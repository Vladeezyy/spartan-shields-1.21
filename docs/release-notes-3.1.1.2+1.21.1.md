## Spartan Shields 3.1.1.2 for NeoForge 1.21.1

Unofficial NeoForge 1.21.1 port of [Spartan Shields](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/spartan-shields)
3.1.1 by **ObliviousSpartan**. Java 21, NeoForge 21.1.197 or newer, no other mods needed (client and server).

### What's new
- **Six languages**: Russian, Ukrainian, German, Spanish and Brazilian Portuguese join English (the original had only
  English). They are machine translations; corrections are welcome in the issues.
- **License**: the port is now released under the GNU LGPL 3.0 only. The original's Apache License 2.0 text stays in
  `licenses/LICENSE-APACHE`, and every license text is inside the jar. Earlier releases keep the license they came
  out with.
- Gameplay is the same as 3.1.1.1 (the fixes for issues #1, #4, #6, #7 and #10 of the original are in it).

### Tested
Client scene in English, Russian and German (all checks pass, the longer German tooltips fit); dedicated servers
NeoForge 21.1.197 and 21.1.259. Stress and compatibility runs as for 3.1.1.1 (Mekanism, Ender IO, Sodium + Iris,
Lithium, FerriteCore, ModernFix, JEI, Jade).

### Known issues
- Botania has no 1.21.1 release yet, so the Botania shields are untested in game.

Full list of changes: [PORTLOG.md](https://github.com/Vladeezyy/spartan-shields-1.21/blob/main/PORTLOG.md).
